title: "What are probing classifiers?"
url: "https://blog.bluedot.org/p/what-are-probing-classifiers"
---

Train a small classifier on a model's internal activations and see what it can predict. If the probe recovers a concept, the representation encodes it somewhere — though a probe finding information is not the same as the model using it.
